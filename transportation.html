Transportation

Getting to the Island

Air

Most visitors arrive by air. Taniti is served by a small airport that can accomodate small jets and propeller planes. Taniti is expanding their airport so larger jets can land in the future.

Cruise Ship

Some visitors arrive by a small cruise ship that docks in Yellow Leaf Bay. The cruise ship only stays for one night and returns once per week, so plan accordingly.

Getting Around the Island

Bus

Public buses serve Taniti City from 5 AM to 11 PM every day. Private buses serve the rest of the island. Buses are an inexpensive and readily available way to get around the island.

Car

Taxi services are available in Taniti City. Rental cars are also available at a rental agency near the airport. Taxi and rental car services offer a more private form of transportation around the island.

Bicycle

Bicycles and helmets are available to rent from several vendors on the island. Helmets are required by law to be worn at all times when biking.

On Foot

Taniti City is fairly flat and very walkable. The area surrounding Merriton Landing, where many visitors stay, is also easy to explore on foot.
