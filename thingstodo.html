Things to Do

Outdoor Fun

Beaches

Taniti's white, sandy beaches are a popular attraction for most visitors. Beyond laying in the sun or hiking on the beach, you can also go snorkeling or charter a fishing tour.

Rainforest

Tahiti's rainforest is great for hiking and exploring nature. For a thrill, visitors can go zip-lining through the rainforest as well.

Volcano

Within its mountainous interior, Tahiti is home to a small, active volcano. The volcano is a popular destination amongst visitors.

Golf

Taniti is in the process of developing a nine-hole golf course. The course should be fully operational by next year.

Guided Tours

Taniti offers several guided tour options. With the assistance of a guide, you can explore the island by bus, boat, or even by helicopter.

Indoor Fun

History Museum

Check out the local history museum and immerse yourself in the rich culture of Taniti.

Art Galleries

Discover the vibrant art of Taniti at a local art gallery. If you'd like to support a local artist, some galleries have prints available for purchase.

Arcades

Release your inner child and play some games at a local arcade.

Bowling

Have some spare time? Check out a local bowling alley.

Movies

Sit back, relax, and catch a local flick at a movie theater.
