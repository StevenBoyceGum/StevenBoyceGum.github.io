Lodging

Hostel

If you're feeling social and looking for inexpensive lodging, consider staying at one of Taniti's hostels. Hostels are a great way to meet fellow travelers and enjoy a sense of community during your stay on Taniti.

Resort

Taniti offers one 4-star resort. The resort is large and accomodating. All inclusive packages are available. If you're looking for a luxurious lodging option, consider staying at the resort.

Hotel

There are many small, family-owned hotels on Taniti. No matter where you are staying on the island, there is likely a nearby hotel.

Bed & Breakfast

There are a growing number of bed & breakfasts on Taniti. If you're looking for a more personal experience with the locals, a bed & breakfast is the way to go.

All types of lodging are strictly regulated and regularly inspected by the Tanitian government.
