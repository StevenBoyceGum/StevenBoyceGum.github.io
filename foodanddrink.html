Food & Drink

Grocery Stores

Taniti has two supermarkets and two smaller grocery stores. There is also a convenience store that is open 24 hours a day.

Restaurants

Taniti currently has 10 restaurants. Five of them serve mostly local fish and rice. Three serve American style meals. The remaining two serve Pan-Asian cuisine.

Pubs

There are several pubs located on the island. If you're looking to try some local beer offerings, Taniti does have one microbrewery as well.

Nightclubs

Taniti's nightclubs offer an exciting atmosphere where you can grab a cocktail and head out on the dance floor. Don't forget to bring your dancing shoes!
