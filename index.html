Welcome to Taniti

Your island adventure awaits...

Taniti is a small, tropical island in the Pacific. Despite its small size, the Tanitian terrain is varied. There are sandy and rocky beaches, a small harbor, lush rainforests, and a mountainous interior that includes a small, active volcano. Taniti has an indigenous population of about 20,000. Until a recent increase in tourism, most of the Tanitian economy was dominated by fishing and agriculture.
